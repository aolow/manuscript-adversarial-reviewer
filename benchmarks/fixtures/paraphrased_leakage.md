# Internal prediction with an implicit boundary failure

## Abstract

Our model predicts response in the development population.

## Methods

We studied 40 patients.
Outcome-guided marker ranking used the complete cohort; only afterward were individuals assigned to training or test partitions.
An independent external cohort of 25 patients was also evaluated.
Confidence intervals were estimated by patient bootstrap.
Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate correction was used.
