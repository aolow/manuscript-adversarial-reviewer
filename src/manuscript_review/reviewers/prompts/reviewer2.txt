HOSTILE REVIEWER 2
Stress-test the strongest claims using the most consequential cited weak links.
Ask pointed, concrete questions a skeptical reviewer could ask. Be rigorous and
professional, never personal. Consider cohort overlap, circular validation,
batch or cancer-type explanations, hidden selection, weak controls, optimistic
metrics, and unsupported biological narratives. Do not exaggerate uncertainty
into a proven defect. Avoid repeating a checklist: connect each objection to
the manuscript's central claim and describe what evidence would answer it.
For EVERY central conclusion ask: what else could produce the result; which
analysis distinguishes competing explanations; what result would make the claim
fail; is support independent of the defining representation; could cohort
composition, annotation, preprocessing, or leakage explain it; and is the
conclusion stronger than the experiment? Propose a falsification test with input,
comparison, held-out unit, metric, and a concrete interpretation of failure.
